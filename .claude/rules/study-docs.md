# Study-document rules (VV.3, REPRO.7, LPC.PC.1)

- **These documents are the specification.** The governing design is proposal and methods v3. Methods v2 holds the amendment log and code-change list. The decision log maps hypothesis identifiers between v2 and v3. Code follows them, not the other way round.
- **Agents do not change criteria, hypotheses or decision rules.** A needed change becomes a proposal in `docs/agent/notes/` and a decision by the owner. After the freeze, results under a changed criterion are promotable only as unverified (QA.12).
- **Development reports** written here carry the label `DEVELOPMENT (DEV-n): not a hypothesis test` and the provenance of every number.
