Verification of the estimator's iterative stage (NVVUQ.10); not a hypothesis test; no verdict; not in the validation gate. The stopping criterion is a fixed 15 Levenberg–Marquardt iterations with no tolerance stop. c is the final cost ‖X_t − G′(û)‖², the sum of D squared terms in the units of σ_x (the noise standard deviation is ρ). The first-order reference for an estimate that converged in the correct basin is c/ρ² ~ χ²(dof) with dof = D − m delay coordinates beyond the state (4 at D = 7: median 3.36, p95 9.49). 'Accepted steps' counts every iteration whose candidate cost was strictly lower, round-off accepts after convergence included, so it is an upper bound on the productive steps and not the number of iterations to convergence. A fraction with no decrease above 0 indicates a failure (non-finite cost, corrupted Jacobian, or a stationary start), not slow convergence. 'Late progress' is the fraction of estimates with an accepted step in the last 3 iterations that lowered the cost by more than 1e-6 relative (or left an infinite cost): a screen for an estimate still moving at the budget. The fraction with c/ρ² above the 0.999 quantile of χ²(dof) (18.47 for dof 4) is 0.001 under the reference (a converged estimate in the correct basin with an exact model); it also counts wrong-basin estimates, estimates still unconverged at the budget, and model error (a misspecified model gives a non-central χ²: in a probe at 5% misspecification 100% exceeded at ρ = 0.01 and 23% at ρ = 0.05). Infinite residuals count in this fraction and also in the non-finite residuals column, so the two overlap. It is printed as a diagnostic, with no gate or verdict role. Neither column estimates the iterative error of any reported quantity (NVVUQ.9).
