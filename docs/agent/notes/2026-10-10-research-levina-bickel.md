## Summary for WU-0005.3

1. Printed estimator (Eq. 8) is the k-1 form (the MLE). The k-2 form is described in the paper as the asymptotically unbiased variant, with E = m and Var = m^2/(k-3) (Eq. 10). The code's `(k-2)/sum` over k-1 log-ratios is that variant. The unbiasedness claim is only first-order, asymptotic, and under the exact-Poisson assumption. Needs k >= 4 for a finite variance.
2. Published aggregation: arithmetic mean of m_hat_k(X_i) over points (Eq. 9), then mean over k in [k1,k2]. The code uses the median over states, which is a departure from the paper. The median is not unbiased for m even in the Poisson model, because 1/m_hat is Gamma-distributed (skewed). WU-0005.3 should state this as a deliberate departure, or test mean, inverse-of-mean-inverse (MacKay-Ghahramani form) and median against sets of known dimension.
3. MacKay-Ghahramani: averaging of inverses is reported by secondary sources; primary text not read. Do not cite the comment's content directly until it is retrieved.
4. Bibliographic caution: the NIPS 17 proceedings volume appeared in 2005 (conference December 2004). Cite as "Levina and Bickel (2005), NIPS 17" or "2004/2005" consistently. Page numbers 777-784 come from a search-result summary and were not confirmed on the PDF.
