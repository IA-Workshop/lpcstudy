## Q1. How the W/U ulp change reaches the H1a fit leaves

### Q1.1 The computation (code at c80f908)

- **Inputs.** Per noise level ρ and library size n_lib, the column median over the test-sweep states of the squared analog seed error, m_j. The leaf `median_seed_by_n_lib` stores √m_j. Distances are whitened, so W and U enter here.
- **Fit.** `_fit_seed_law` fits log m_j = log(c² n_j^(−2/d) + f²). It uses `curve_fit` with bounds, which is `least_squares(method="trf")` with the scipy defaults: ftol = xtol = gtol = 1e-8 and `jac="2-point"`. The parameters are θ = (log c², 2/d, log f²).
- **Leaves.** `fitted_inverse_dim` = (2/d)/2. `floor_rms` = f. `ci_inverse_dim` is the 2.5% and 97.5% percentiles of the same fit over B moving-block bootstrap resamples. `ratio` and `ratio_ci` are the same fit divided by `inverse_dim_reference` (a direct mean).
- **Sizes.** Smoke has 2 library sizes. Pilot and full have 5.

**Observed on the kernel pair** (`q2_scale.log`, `q1_reproduce.log`). The input changes are 1.7e-15 and 1.6e-14 relative on m_j. The outputs change by 6.8e-8 (`fitted_inverse_dim`), 2.93e-7 and 7.3e-9 (`ci_inverse_dim`) of s = max(\|lo\|, \|hi\|) = 0.6469, and by 2.5e-7 relative (`floor_rms`). That is an amplification of about 10⁷. The second S3 pair gives the same numbers to all digits.

### Q1.2 Decomposition

For two correct runs A and B with inputs y = log m differing by δy:

Δθ = [θ\*(y_A) − θ\*(y_B)] + [e_A − e_B],

where θ\* is the exact least-squares minimiser and e = θ_solver − θ\* is the stopping error.

1. **Exact minimiser: well conditioned** (`q1_pilot_sensitivity.log`).
   - On the 12 P0/P1 pilot cells (5 sizes), J has rank 3 with σ_min = 0.073–0.155.
   - dθ\*/dy, by central differences on fits solved to 1e-15, gives \|Δ(1/d)\| ≤ 1.1e-13·s for \|δy\| ≤ 3.2e-14 (2× the kernel-pair input change).
   - **An exact fit would pass the cp 1e-12 rule.**
2. **Smoke: not identified** (`q1_reproduce.log`).
   - With 2 sizes and 3 parameters, J is 2×3 (σ = 9.34, 0.256), and the zero-cost fits form a curve. Fixing 2/d at 0.988, 1.097 or 1.207 gives exact fits (cost ≤ 1e-31) with floor 0.0152, 0.0297 or 0.0370.
   - The reported 1/d = 0.5487 is selected by the starting guess and the solver path (gtol stop, 23 evaluations, cost 2.5e-28), not by the data. No conditioning bound exists.
   - **Side finding (report only):** the smoke H1a 1/d, ratio and interval are plumbing values. They must not calibrate any H1a tolerance. The d3 class-(a) maximum (Q2) is exactly such a leaf. Pilot and full have 5 sizes and are identified.
3. **The mechanism: the finite-difference Jacobian** (`q1_flip_bound.log`, `q1_ftol_theory.log`).
   - scipy's 2-point Jacobian uses h = √ε·max(1, \|x\|) ≈ 1.5e-8. The residual r = model − y is formed at the size of \|y\| ≈ 3–6, so each J entry carries a rounding error ‖δJ‖ ≈ √m·2u·max\|y\|/h ≈ 1e-7. That error is a pseudo-random function of the last bits of y.
   - With a nonzero residual, the Gauss–Newton fixed point moves by about ‖J⁺‖·‖δJ‖·‖r‖/σ_min. On the pilot cells that is ≤ 5.1e-7·s.
   - **Test:** the ulp experiment, repeated with an analytic Jacobian, amplifies by only 3.6e-14 to 7.5e-14·s. With the production 2-point Jacobian it amplifies by 7.9e-9 to 5.7e-8·s (4 pilot cells and smoke). The ×10⁷ comes from the FD Jacobian, not from the conditioning.
   - **Without a change of stopping iteration** (2400 point perturbations, none changed the stop), the measured maximum is 1.1e-7·s. Over 9600 resample-like perturbations (134 of which changed the stop) the maximum is 2.0e-7·s (`q1_pilot_sensitivity.log`).
4. **Stopping flip.**
   - The pilot fits stop on ftol after 6–10 evaluations. Near the minimum, F − F\* ≲ ftol·F/(1 − c²) for a linear rate c, so \|e\| ≲ √ftol·‖r‖·‖J⁺_row‖. That is ≤ 1.92e-5·s on the 12 cells, and the observed \|e\| is ≤ 0.12 of it (max 2.4e-6·s).
   - A marginal stopping test can stop A and B one step apart, so \|e_A − e_B\| ≤ \|e_A\| + \|e_B\|.
   - **Measured flip size** (refit at ftol/2 and 2·ftol, the neighbouring stops): ≤ 1.44e-6·s at the 12 points. On 480 resample-like inputs (each median jittered by 5% log-SD, which spans the pilot interval widths) it is ≤ 7.4e-6·s, with a p99 of 5.0e-6.
   - **Flip frequency** under ulp perturbations: 0 in 2400 at the points, and 134 in 9600 (1.4%) for the resample-like inputs.
   - **Interval endpoints.** A percentile endpoint is 1-Lipschitz in the sup-norm of the resample values. So the same per-fit bound applies to `ci_inverse_dim`, with e taken over the resamples: observed max 1.16e-5·s on the resample-like inputs.

### Q1.3 The bound, and what it says about each candidate rule

For two correct runs (pilot-shaped inputs; full is no worse, because ‖r‖ does not grow with n):

\|Δ\|/s ≤ 1.5e-13 (conditioning) + 2·5.1e-7 (FD fixed point) + 2·1.92e-5 (stopping) ≈ **4.0e-5** (a priori).

The measured values are 7.4e-6 (flip) and 2.0e-7 (no flip).

| Candidate rule for the H1a fit leaves | Against the bound | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| cp chain 1e-12·\|ref\| (the current strict reading) | Fails correct code by 5 to 7 orders | Wrong premise (N1 covers direct arithmetic, not a stopped iterative solver) |
| 1e-5·s (tightest power of ten below 1e-4) | Below the a-priori bound; 1.35× above the largest measured flip | Not derivable with a safety factor |
| **1e-4·s (the A6 QoI rule, class (a))** | Safety factor 2.5 a priori, 13.5 measured, ≥ 500 without a flip | **Holds**: the tightest rule with a derived margin |
| `floor_rms`, class (g) at the (b)–(g) tolerance 1e-4·\|ref\| | Measured flip ≤ 6.3e-7, stopping error ≤ 6.8e-7 (points; it has no interval) | Holds, safety factor ≥ 70 |

The same analysis covers `ratio` and `ratio_ci`, which already fall under the 1e-4·s rule. **So this note also checks the A6 QoI rule on the verdict-bearing H1a leaves:** its a-priori margin is 2.5. It is thin for the same reason as OF-1's proxy fit (the √ftol stop; 1.7× there), and I add it to that flag as information.

**Tiers.**
- In T1 and T3 these leaves are normally bitwise equal: the analog search, whitening and fit run on the host, and W and U are equal. They move only in T2.
- A cross-host T1 (WU-0016) could also move them if numpy's SIMD `exp`/`log` differ between CPUs. That is a hypothesis, not checked.
- The recommended rule is the same in T1 to T3.

### Q1.4 Recommendation (one): re-tag the H1a fit outputs

- **The change, in `compare_runs` only.** An explicit override, placed before the chain rule, for `systems/*/H1a/by_noise/*/{fitted_inverse_dim, ci_inverse_dim/[]}`, making them class (a) under the QoI rule 1e-4·max(\|lo\|, \|hi\|) of `ci_inverse_dim`. Plus `systems/*/H1a/by_noise/*/floor_rms` as class (g) under the (b)–(g) tolerance. The reason printed beside them: "tolerance-stopped fit (curve_fit ftol 1e-8, 2-point Jacobian); note 2026-10-14-math-wu0003-9-q1".
- **Do not edit `compare_pilot`'s tag.** Its meaning, upstream dependence, is correct: these leaves do depend only on the cp chain. What fails is the 1e-12 premise.
- **d3 is unchanged.** The class-(a) maximum stays 2.9357e-7 (`ratio_ci/[0]`; `ci_inverse_dim/[0]` is 2.9321e-7). The (b)–(g) maximum stays 4.99e-6 (`floor_rms` is 2.52e-7).
- **Status: loosening, flagged, not adopted.**
  - For these leaves the rule goes from 1e-12·\|ref\| to 1e-4·s, and the kernel-pair evidence run already exists. A6 never names these leaves as cp chain, and N1 does not cover them. Even so, a wider tolerance after a result is the owner's decision (PRIME.2).
  - Proposed text for B4: "**OF-7.** The H1a fit outputs (`fitted_inverse_dim`, `ci_inverse_dim`, `floor_rms`) are governed by the A6 QoI rule (class (a); `floor_rms` class (g)) instead of the cp-chain 1e-12, on the derived bound of note 2026-10-14-math-wu0003-9-q1 §Q1.3. Working default: cp 1e-12."
- **Working default until the owner rules.**
  - The tool keeps the strict 1e-12. Expected effect: every T2 pair (R4 against R3; WU-0016 cross-host) fails on up to 24 H1a leaves at pilot size (3 systems × 2 ρ × 4 leaves) with code that is correct.
  - I suggest the tool print the pointer line above beside these failures. That is a report change; the exit code is unchanged. The main session then reads them under this note, not as a DEF.
- **Option for the owner, not now.** An analytic Jacobian and ftol = xtol = gtol ≈ 1e-14 in `_fit_seed_law` would make the fit reproducible to about 1e-13·s and would restore the cp rule. But it changes numbers (not bitwise-neutral; A2 step 6), so it belongs after the rehearsal, if at all, with a new DEV reference.
- **Tightening, adoptable now (stricter, no flag).**
  - `H1a/by_noise/*/median_seed_by_n_lib/*` and `H1a/inverse_dim_reference` are direct cp-chain arithmetic: a median of whitened distances, and a mean of log distance ratios. They are untagged today, so they get class (g) at 1e-4.
  - On the kernel pair they move by 8.2e-15 and 2.2e-16 relative. The cp 1e-12 rule fits them with a margin ≥ 120.
