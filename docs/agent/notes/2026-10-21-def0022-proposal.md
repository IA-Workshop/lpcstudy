## What the finding is

The second part of H2a, H2a(ii), tests a partial correlation against the band (-0.1, 0.1) (v3 section 2; interval strictly inside). Its interval is wide at the study's sample size, so it can be refuted (an interval that excludes the band) but essentially cannot pass. v3 section 7 already pre-registers this in its precision list (`docs/study/proposal_and_methods_v3.md` line 363): "H2a's second part can be refuted but essentially cannot pass: that would need a partial correlation within about 0.01 of zero." DEF-0022 (`docs/agent/records/defects.md`, status line of 2026-10-18 that supersedes the earlier one) is open, tracked and not blocking: the reference was corrected, the power finding was "not accepted by any owner answer", and its wording is not on any limitation list. **Why it is open (`defects.md` line 271, which supersedes the earlier line 264 "power finding accepted as a pre-registered limitation"):** the full-size check this defect asked for (the full-size report of WU-0003, `docs/agent/records/work/WU-0003-R3R6-report-R6.txt` lines 143-152) found "v3 precision not reproduced" on 5 of 6 H2a(ii) parts, so v3 line 363 ("within about 0.01 of zero") "does not describe the full-size evidence part by part". The qualitative finding (can be refuted, essentially cannot pass) is pre-registered; the projected margin is not reproduced. The remaining owner action named there is to accept the finding and its wording, with the WU-0003.3 report, and add it to the limitations list. The sealed run shows the same: the stored Rossler interior interval [-0.04826, 0.1662] is wider than the band (-0.1, 0.1) (traced endpoints; upper minus lower is more than 0.2), so that part could not pass at any estimate.
