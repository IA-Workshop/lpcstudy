- Evidence: the superseding status line `defects.md:271` is open because "'v3 precision not reproduced' on 5 of 6 H2a(ii) parts" at full size (R6 report, `WU-0003-R3R6-report-R6.txt` lines 143-152), so "v3 line 363 ('within about 0.01 of zero') does not describe the full-size evidence part by part". Its owner action names "the WU-0003.3 report". The proposal quotes only "not accepted by any owner answer". Option 1 then argues "already pre-registered in v3 section 7 … so it is not a post hoc change", and the draft status line reads "power finding accepted as a pre-registered limitation". That is the wording of `defects.md:264`, which line 271 superseded for exactly this reason. Wordings A and B say "as the precision list of v3 §7 states", without saying that its quantitative projection was not borne out. The sealed run shows the same thing. Reviewer arithmetic on the traced section 5 endpoints gives 0.1 minus the half-width at about +0.026 (Lorenz-63 zero), +0.005 (Lorenz-63 interior), +0.012 (Rossler zero), −0.007 (Rossler interior), +0.025 (Chua zero) and +0.005 (Chua interior). The Rossler interior interval [−0.04826, 0.1662] is wider than the band itself, so that part could not pass at any estimate.
- Why it matters: LPC.PC.14 and INTPR.10 apply to a promotion decision. The owner would be asked to rule on a framing the register has already rejected once, without the evidence that rejected it.

Reword option 1 to say the qualitative finding is pre-registered but the projected margin ("about 0.01") is not reproduced part by part. Optionally state the part whose interval is wider than the band, labeled as arithmetic on traced endpoints. Make A and B say "as v3 §7 anticipated; its projected margin is not reproduced part by part (DEF-0022)" or similar, then edit the constant, rebuild and run `check`.
