## 1. The exact model of the per-state MSE

**What is averaged** (`pipeline.score_rules`). For a rule ρ, test state i (in time order) and evaluation draw d = 1..E, at H_ref:

X^ρ_id = (w^ρ_id e_C,id + (1 − w^ρ_id) e_P,id)²,  a^ρ_i = (1/E) Σ_d X^ρ_id = `core/state_mse/<ρ>[:, ref]`.

- e_C and e_P are the post-clip evaluation errors.
- **Weights** (`pipeline.rule_weights`):
  - A0 has w = 1 and A1 has w = 0.
  - A2 has the global weight at H_ref, constant over states and draws.
  - A3 is the predictor network on `ev.inputs`, the draw's noisy delay vector.
  - A4 is `closed_form_weight(eps_hat², delta_hat²)` from `proxies.seeds(ev)`: the draw's recurrence distance R and its estimate's ν, among others.
  - A5 is the regime weight of `deployable_profile(ev)` of the draw.
- So for A3, A4 and A5 the weight w_id and the errors (e_C,id, e_P,id) are functions of **the same** noise draw (query noise, analog retrieved, LM estimate). They are dependent.

**Hypothesis H-iid.** Given state i and the fitted rules, the triples (w_id, e_C,id, e_P,id), d = 1..E, are i.i.d.
- *Basis (code):* `unit_noise`, `verify_unit` and `synth_unit` are independent per (i, d), and the fitted rules are fixed within a run.
- The oracle draws come from the same `forecast_draws` with independent noise, so their (e_C, e_P) law given i is the evaluation law, and they are independent of the evaluation draws.
- *Numerical check:* §7.

**Notation.** For each rule ρ and state i:
- μ_i = E[X_id | i] and τ_i² = Var(X_id | i);
- V_i(w) = w²E_C + (1 − w)²E_P + 2w(1 − w)C, with the true conditional moments;
- h_i(w) = E[X | w, i] − V_i(w), the dependence function. It is 0 if w is independent of the errors. Expanded:

  h_i(w) = w²(E[e_C² | w] − E_C) + (1 − w)²(E[e_P² | w] − E_P) + 2w(1 − w)(E[e_C e_P | w] − C)

**The draw-noise component as a function of E.** Under H-iid alone, with no assumption on the dependence:
- E[a_i | i] = μ_i and Var(a_i | i) = τ_i²/E, exactly.
- The decomposition τ_i² = Var(V_i(w) + h_i(w)) + E[Var(X | w)] shows that the dependence enters the **size** of τ_i², through h, but not the **1/E law**.

For a criterion, the influence is linear in the per-state values: ψ_i = (a^F_i − a^S_i) − mean for H2c, and (a^F_i − R a^S_i)/b̄ for H2d and H3c (first order, with fixed R and b̄). The per-draw contribution is ψ_id = X^F_id − X^S_id, or (X^F_id − R X^S_id)/b̄. With BV(x) = xᵀQx the block variance (b = 5):

E BV(ψ(a_E)) = L_μ + S_τ/E,

where:
- L_μ = E BV(ψ(μ)) is the state part;
- S_τ = Σ_i Q_ii Var(ψ_id | i) is the per-draw part. The draws of different states are independent, so only the diagonal of Q enters;
- Var(ψ_id | i) = τ_F² + τ_S² − 2Cov(X^F, X^S | i) for H2c. The cross-rule covariance on the same draw is large.

Hence κ_E² = (L_μ + S_τ/E_full)/(L_μ + S_τ/E_run) ∈ [E_run/E_full, 1].
- **Correct**, given two hypotheses: H-iid, and the rules held fixed. A refit at full size is outside κ, as is "conditional on K".
- It is exact for H2c and first order for the ratios.

**The tool's residual.** V̂_i = (1/E) Σ_d V̂_i(w_id), where V̂_i uses the oracle sample moments (M = 200). Write V̂_i = ν_i + c̄_i + δ_i, with:
- ν_i = E[V_i(w_d)], so that b_i = μ_i − ν_i = E[h_i(w_d)];
- c̄_i the mean over the E_run weights of V_i(w_d) − ν_i: variance σ_c,i²/E, and covariance γ_i/E with the draw noise ε̄_i = a_i − μ_i;
- δ_i the oracle-moment error: mean 0, variance about τ_i²/M.

Then:
- E[a_i − V̂_i | i] = b_i, **independent of E** (the reviewer's point, confirmed);
- Var(a_i − V̂_i | i) = (τ_i² + σ_c,i² − 2γ_i)/E + O(τ_i²/M).

## 2. What the tool estimates (first order; O(b/n) terms of BV dropped)

With L_x = E BV(ψ(x)), L_xy the cross form, and S_c and S_γ defined like S_τ (Q_ii-weighted sums of the influence-level σ_c² and γ):

| | Tool | Truth |
| --- | --- | --- |
| State part | E V_s = L_ν + L_δ + S_c/E_run | L_μ = L_ν + L_b + 2L_νb |
| Draw part at E_run | E V_d = L_b + L_δ + (S_τ + S_c − 2S_γ)/E_run | S_τ/E_run |

The tool's estimand is (E V_s + r E V_d)/(E V_s + E V_d), with r = E_run/E_full = 1/2. It equals κ_E² only if b ≡ 0, c ≡ 0 and δ ≡ 0. The cases:

1. **Weights constant per state** (A0, A1, A2): b = c = 0. Only δ is left. It enters the numerator as (1 + r)L_δ and the denominator as 2L_δ, with L_δ ≈ (E_run/M)(S_τ/E_run) = 1% of the draw part. So |Δκ| ≤ 0.004. No criterion here is of this kind.
2. **Independence** (h ≡ 0): b = 0 and S_γ = S_c, since Cov(X, V(w)) = Var(V(w)). The tool's κ² is too large by (1 − r)(S_c/E_run)/(L_μ + S_τ/E_run). **Upward**: the weight-draw noise of the E_run weights is counted as state variance. This is the reviewer's second mechanism.
3. **Dependence** (b ≠ 0). The numerator changes by −(1 − r)L_b − 2L_νb + r(S_c − 2S_γ + …)/E_run, and the denominator by −2L_νb + ….
   - If b is uncorrelated with ν, the move of L_b from the state part to the draw part biases κ **down**.
   - If b ∝ −ν (an adaptive rule gains in proportion to the level), V_s ≈ L_ν > L_μ biases it **up**.
   - The sign is not determined.
4. **Finite n.** V_s and V_d are non-negative, so κ̂ ∈ [√r, 1] always. When the draw share is near 1 (κ near √r), which is where every mechanistic world put H2d and H3c, the sampling SD of κ̂ (0.01–0.07) turns into an **upward** bias of the same order.

## 4. The half draws, and the estimator the tool should compute

**The oracle halves in the present dump.** For a fixed w, V̂^A_i(w) − V̂^B_i(w) has variance 4τ_i²(w)/M. So (M/4)(V̂^A_i(w) − V̂^B_i(w))² is an unbiased per-state estimate of the per-draw variance, **for draw-constant weights only**. The oracle draws carry no A3/A4/A5 weight. Every H2c/H2d/H3c part contains A3, A4 or A5, so the halves give no unbiased estimator of κ_E here.

**The needed dump addition** (new sub-task, `lpcstudy/dump.py`, owner review): `core/cp_err_href` and `core/plp_err_href`, the post-clip evaluation errors at H_ref, each (n, E) float64.
- Size: 64 KB per system at full size.
- Then X^ρ_id = (w e_C + (1 − w) e_P)² with `weight_href` (A0 w = 1, A1 w = 0).
- Check, as in the present tool: mean_d X^ρ_id reproduces `state_mse/<ρ>[:, ref]` to 1e-12 relative, else MISMATCH and no use.

**The estimator (half-split of the evaluation draws).**
1. For E even, let a^{ρ,1}_i and a^{ρ,2}_i be the MSEs of draws [0, E/2) and [E/2, E).
2. Form the contrast:
   - H2c: h_i = ½[(a^{F,1} − a^{F,2}) − (a^{S,1} − a^{S,2})];
   - H2d and H3c: h_i = [(a^{F,1} − a^{F,2}) − R̂(a^{S,1} − a^{S,2})]/(2b̄), with R̂ and b̄ from the full a.
3. Then:

   κ̂² = 1 − (1 − E_run/E_full) · BV(h)/BV(ψ(a)).

- **Why it is unbiased.** Under H-iid, h_i has conditional mean 0. Its conditional variance equals that of the per-draw part of ψ_i(a): Var((Ā₁ − Ā₂)/2) = σ²/E = Var(Ā − μ). It is independent across states, so E BV(h) = S_τ/E_run. That is exactly the per-draw part of E BV(ψ(a)) = L_μ + S_τ/E_run, including BV's finite-n factor. The state part does not enter h.
- No assumption on the weight-error dependence is made. The only bias is the O(1/n) ratio bias: −0.002 to −0.005 in κ² (toy).
- In the mechanistic worlds its population form is within about 2 SE of the truth in the light-tailed scenarios (|bias| ≤ 0.004 in S3, S4, S9–S11). It is noisier, but not shown biased beyond Monte Carlo error, in the heavy-tailed ones (S12 H2c +0.08 ± 0.05).
- **Precision.**
  - SD(κ̂²) per run at n = 500 is 0.07–0.30 in the light and moderate worlds. It is 0.4–2.1 for the ratio criteria in the heavy-tailed ones (S6, S7, S8, S12, S13).
  - When κ is near √r, 30–53% of the runs fall outside [1/2, 1].
  - A delete-a-block jackknife (20 contiguous blocks of 25 states; deterministic, no random number) gives median SE(κ̂²) 0.05–0.10. κ̂² ± 2 SE covers κ² in 0.83–0.96 of the runs (`r2_jack.log`; lowest for H2c in S2).
- **Recommended use.**
  - Print κ̂² raw, with its SE, and count it if it falls outside [1/2, 1]. Never clip it silently.
  - Project with the range [√max(1/2, κ̂² − 2SE), √min(1, κ̂² + 2SE)]: the intersection of two valid ranges, printed as such.
  - At n = 500 this will often be close to [0.707, 1] anyway.
  - f2 should then be redesigned by the planner, in a world with per-draw weights and dependence on. Coverage of the ±2 SE range is the natural criterion, since 0.83 is below 0.95. f2's present bound, seed and R are not to be changed.

**What the tool should compute until then.** The range [√(E_run/E_full), 1] = [0.7071, 1] for H2c, H2d and H3c, through the existing FALLBACK path (printed, counted). The present κ̂ may be printed for information, labelled "not an estimator of κ_E (R2; biased, sign undetermined)", and never used in a projection.
