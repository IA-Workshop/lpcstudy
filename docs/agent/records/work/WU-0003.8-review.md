## Findings

**R1 (medium-high; silent, favourable direction). A non-finite half-width yields a reading instead of "not computable".**
- **Where:** `tools/rehearsal_diagnostics.py` 1235–1249 (`_d5_primary`), 1352–1366 (`section_d6`), 622–628 (`three_way_reading`) and 738–756 (`full_halfwidth`).
- **The defect:**
  - `percentile_interval` returns `(-inf, inf)` once failed resamples reach `floor((B − 1)a) + 1`, which is 5 of 500 at 1 − 0.05/3.
  - `save_json` writes them with `allow_nan=True`, so `range_ci` can reach the tool as `[-Infinity, Infinity]`.
  - Then `hw_full = inf`, and `three_way_reading` returns **"below 10%"**, so the report prints "budget row 1 may be stated below 10%".
  - Scratch probe: `three_way_reading(0.03, 0.004, 0.1·inf)` returns `below 10%`. A NaN half-width gives "not determined", again with no "not computable".
- **Why it matters:** PRIME.4/NVVUQ.11. This is exactly the favourable reading PF-5 guards against.
- **Fix:** if either interval endpoint, `hw` or `hw_full` is not finite, record `rep.nc(... "half-width not finite: <ci>")` and print no three-way or point reading. Add a unit test with `range_ci = [-inf, inf]` and one with NaN, each expecting "not computable" and exit 2.

**R2 (medium; criterion dropped under a wrong label). H1d is computable from the schema but is listed as "outside the schema".**
- **Where:** `OUTSIDE_SCHEMA` (line 125) and the report line at 1450.
- **The defect:**
  - H1d's three tested pairs are Spearman correlations of the deployable proxies (first evaluation draw) with `eps_s`, `delta` and `Λ̄` (`analysis.h1d_proxies`).
  - The dump holds `core/profile (n, E, 4)`, whose columns are monotone transforms of the proxies (`allocation.deployable_profile`; Spearman is invariant to them), and `core/eps_s`, `core/delta` and `core/Lambda_bar`.
  - M4.1 gives the H1d influence (the Spearman rank-product influence).
  - Only H1a (per-state analog seed error per library size) and H1c (per-state event horizons) are absent from `lpcstudy.states/1`.
- **Why it matters:** plan row .8 says "every series is listed … never skipped", and C8 D1 says "each criterion's influence series". H1d is reported with exit 0 as if it were impossible.
- **Fix:** implement the H1d series:
  - three parts, plus the reported N̂ pair if wanted;
  - a Spearman influence function;
  - a reproduction check against `H1d/.../spearman` (verify the leaf path);
  - a c1-type test: the Gateaux derivative of the weighted-rank Spearman, or an independent closed form on a tie-free sample.

  Alternatively, ask the planner to exclude H1d explicitly. Keep H1a and H1c as outside the schema (see Q1).

**R3 (medium; acceptance overstated). c6 checks the one-line helper against the same expression, and the D5 estimator as composed is never value-tested.**
- **Where:** `tests/unit/test_tools_rehearsal_diagnostics.py` 526–545.
- **The defect:**
  - The "script reference" is `0.5*(med a + med b) − med m`. `split_half_shift` is that expression, so the first assertion is the code against itself.
  - The arrays come from a `make_rng` re-implementation, not from the script's `default_rng(20261017)` stream. That is disclosed in the docstring, but the Log says "c1 to c7 met".
  - The path that matters is not tested against any independent value: `h2a_inputs` → `leading_ranges` (200-draw eligibility and segments) → `segment_ranges` for the halves → medians over the eligible states. The end-to-end test asserts only that the reading is one of three strings.
- **Why it matters:** LPC.PC.21. This is the input to the M decision.
- **Fix:**
  - Put the model's `(a, b, m)` into a constructed dump as `core/w_star_A`, `w_star_B` and `w_star` at three horizons ≥ 1 LT.
  - Make most states small at all three horizons and some ineligible (large `E_C` at 1 LT, or small only at the first).
  - Run `section_d5` and compare `delta_sh` and `bias_order_half` with the script's expression over the eligible rows, computed in the test with loops (1e-12).
  - Record the generator substitution as a planner point beside Q8 (the plan's "match the script" cannot be met bitwise under the make_rng rule). Change the Log's acceptance line to say what c6 checks.

**R4 (low-medium; false MISMATCH on correct code). Reproduction-check tolerances are relative to |leaf| with no scale floor.**
- **Where:** `check` (809–827), and the leaves checked at 1e-12 or 1e-9 that can be near 0: Q-N ΔR² (958), H2b(i) mean Spearman (887), the H2a(ii) partial correlations (876), and the H2c means if the two rules are close.
- **The defect:** production computes ΔR² as `R²(F4) − R²(F3)`, and the tool computes it as `mean(gain)/SST`. The rounding difference is about 1e-14 absolute.
- **Scratch probe** (n = 500, synthetic, `make_rng`): at |ΔR²| ≈ 7e-5, 116 of 200 cases exceed 1e-12 relative (worst 2.5e-10). At |ΔR²| ≈ 7e-4 or more, none do.
- **Why it matters:** Q-N is an equivalence criterion around 0 (±0.04), so near-zero gains are the expected case. A MISMATCH exits 1 and reads as "a defect of the dump or of this tool", which would trigger a DEF on correct code.
- **Fix:** scale each tolerance by the size of its terms, as b3 and b4 do:
  - ΔR²: `tol·max(|R²(F3)|, |R²(F4)|, |leaf|)`, or γ_n·(SS₃ + SS₄)/SST;
  - means: `tol·mean|terms|`;
  - partial correlation: an absolute floor justified by the lstsq conditioning.

  State each tolerance in the tool and add a test with ΔR² ≈ 1e-5.

**R5 (low; items skipped without being named). A failure inside a section aborts the rest of that section.**
- **Where:** `section_d5` (1208–1216): if `H2a/range_ci` is absent or the bootstrap fails, the exception leaves `_d5_primary`, and the V4 check (which needs neither) never runs. Only "D5: …" is printed, and the computed Δ_sh is not printed.
- **Also:** `section_d3` (1115–1119): `log_sd(state)` is outside the `try`, so a failed state part aborts the remaining rules. Its non-positive count is also discarded (`[0]`), contrary to PRIME.4.
- **Also:** `section_d6` reads `bootstrap_block` before the `try`.
- **Fix:** wrap each item in its own `attempt`; print Δ_sh before the half-width lookup; report `n_bad` of the state part.

**R6 (low; exit-code contract). Unexpected exceptions exit 1, the MISMATCH code, and lose the report.**
- **Where:** `main` (1523–1532). Only `NotComputable` is caught.
- **The defect:**
  - These raise instead:
    - a corrupt `.npz` (`BadZipFile`);
    - a missing `n_test` or `n_test_sweep` (`KeyError` in `full_halfwidth`);
    - a non-scalar `meta/schema` (`ValueError`);
    - a malformed `ci` (`TypeError`).
  - Python then exits 1, which a caller reads as MISMATCH.
  - `rep.lines` are printed only at the end, so the partial report is lost.
- **Fix:** catch `Exception` in `main`, print the lines gathered so far and the traceback to stderr, and exit with a distinct documented code (3), or 2 with an "internal error" line. Test it with a corrupt `.npz`. This also answers Q15.

**R7 (low; plan reading not aggregated). D1's "every |acf(1)|" and D2's "at least one of 18" are only per system.**
- **Where:** 1017–1021 and 1053–1062.
- **The defect:**
  - B1 7.D1 reads "the φ = 0 rows apply if **every** |acf(1)| ≤ threshold", that is, all series, not only primaries.
  - B1 7.D2 prints "at least one of 18 series 0.97 (pilot), 0.83 (full)".
  - The tool gives a per-system count over primaries and a per-system at-least-one rate (about 0.70 at the pilot). No run-level line matches the plan's statement.
- **Fix:** add a run-level summary after the systems:
  - D1: the count of all series within the threshold, and the overall reading;
  - D2: A5 firings over the 18 (or 19+) primaries, with 1 − ∏(1 − r_i), which reproduces 0.97 and 0.83.

**R8 (low; seed-id guard). The guard checks only one recorded seed id.**
- **Where:** 1437–1442. `provenance.seed_id`, or failing that `config.seed_id`, is checked.
- **Fix:** require every recorded id (`provenance.seed_id`, `provenance.seed_kind == "development"`, `config.seed_id`) to be present and to agree, and refuse otherwise. Test it with a disagreeing pair.

**R9 (owner; seed code). The tool seed and the `--seed` override need an owner ruling.**
- **What it is:** `TOOL_SEED = 20261018` is a new unregistered seed constant that feeds reported statistics: the D2 permutation reading and the D5/D6 SE, and so the three-way reading.
- **The risk:** `--seed`, `--permutations` and `--resamples` let a user re-draw until a reading flips. The seed is printed, but nothing prevents shopping.
- **Fix (owner decision, Q9):** a registered tool seed (or one derived from the run's seed id by `make_rng` labels), with `--seed` either removed or flagged "non-default seed" in every reading it affects. **Needs owner review before freeze** (seed code), on top of the statistics checkpoint already marked.

**R10 (low; c7 coverage). The f_b bins are not tested.**
- **Where:** `WEIGHT_BINS` handling, 1375–1385. The bin edges and their closure (exact 0; (0, 0.1); [0.1, 0.9]; (0.9, 1); exact 1) have no test. The D6 test has f_b = 0 everywhere, and `near_clip` is tested alone.
- **Fix:** a constructed cell with at least one state in each bin and on each edge (w = 0.1, 0.9), with counts and f_b by bin decided by hand.
