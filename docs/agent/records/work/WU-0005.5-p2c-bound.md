### Notation (as in the code; the mathematician confirms in .5a)

Per state: clean query x = G(s) (`delay_vectors(ctx.eval_clean, …)`), library vectors X_j = the noisy admissible delay vectors in `AnalogLibrary.tree.data`, r₁ ≤ r₂ the two smallest distances from x (`library.query(x, 2)`), i₁ the nearest, Δ = r₂ − r₁. Draws r = 1…M: η_r = ρ z_r with z_r the core cell's oracle `unit_noise`; Y_r = the k = 1 analog retrieved for x + η_r.

- **N_M** = `retrieval_spread(Y, ρ)`, the code's N, unchanged (Ȳ = empirical mean over the M draws).
- **A_M** = (ρ²D)⁻¹ mean_r ‖Y_r − X_{i₁}‖²: the spread about the clean analog (the proof's middle term).
- **B_M** = 4(ρ²D)⁻¹ mean_r (r₁ + ‖η_r‖)² 1{‖η_r‖ ≥ Δ/2}: the P2(c) bound on the same draws.
- **B_∞** = the same with the expectation over η ~ N(0, ρ²I_D) in closed form: 4(ρ²D)⁻¹[r₁²G₀ + 2r₁ρG₁ + ρ²G₂], G_j = E[χ_D^j 1{χ_D ≥ Δ/(2ρ)}] = 2^{j/2} Γ((D+j)/2)/Γ(D/2) · Q((D+j)/2, Δ²/(8ρ²)), Q = `scipy.special.gammaincc`.

In exact arithmetic N_M² ≤ A_M ≤ B_M for every state and every M (Huygens, then the per-draw triangle inequality and P2(a)). So on the same draws a violation is a defect, never a tolerance.

### APPLIED-MATHEMATICIAN must decide before execution
- **M1. Ȳ.** The options are (a) the empirical mean over the same M draws, which is the code's N, Methods v2 §M5; (b) X_{i₁}, giving A_M; (c) the population mean E[Y], which cannot be computed. The planner recommends (a) as the checked quantity and (b) as the reported middle term. Confirm Huygens: N_M² = A_M − ‖Ȳ − X_{i₁}‖²/(ρ²D).
- **M2. The expectation.** The options are (a) the same M draws (B_M), deterministic, the pass/fail form with zero violations; (b) the population B_∞ (closed form above); (c) both, with (b) reported only. For (b), derive the Monte Carlo law of B_M − B_∞ from χ moments up to order 4. Also give the expected number of states with N_M² > B_∞ by chance at M = 200: switching is rare-event, so a normal approximation is poor. Note that N_M² is biased low by (M − 1)/M against the population variance of Y.
- **M3. Hypotheses on the code.** Confirm that P2 applies verbatim:
  - η is on the query only, with E‖η‖² = Dρ²;
  - X_j are fixed noisy library vectors, and x is the clean delay vector;
  - retrieval is the exact Euclidean argmin (WU-0005.2);
  - k = 1 only;
  - ties (Δ = 0) are harmless, since the indicator is then always 1.
- **M4. Floating-point slack.** Fix τ_N and κ as numbers, with a derivation. τ_N covers the round-off of `retrieval_spread`'s pairwise mean at M = 200 and M = 30 (smoke). κ covers cKDTree's distance round-off (about 10⁻¹⁵ relative, WU-0005.2 Q3).
- **M5. U2 and U3.** Keep the exact `N == 0.0` on the dyadic library (ROADMAP verbatim), and confirm that U3's τ_N is an added check, not a loosening.
- **M6. The B_∞ formula** and U1's tolerance.
- **M7. The synthetic designs.** U4's Δ/ρ grid and r₁; U5's tightness premise of ≥ 0.9; the non-vacuity count.
- **M8. States.** All three splits of the core cell, as proposed, or the test split only. Should a stress cell be added at ρ = 5% (E1), where switching is common? The planner's default is no: the ROADMAP names the core cell.
- **M9. Reporting.** The tightness summaries, and whether to report the switch-indicator variant (1{Y_r ≠ X_{i₁}} in place of 1{‖η_r‖ ≥ Δ/2}) as a further middle term.

### Risks
- **Thread use.** `cKDTree.query(workers=-1)` uses every core, outside `LPC_THREADS`. S1 makes about 4×10⁵ queries per system. This is a known observation from WU-0005; it is not fixed here.
- **S1 runtime** is dominated by `build_context` (Benettin over 10⁵ steps, plus trajectories). If it exceeds 15 min, stop and amend the plan; do not shrink M or the state count after a result is seen.
- **Observation.** For a state that never switches, N is about 10⁻¹² rather than 0 (centring round-off). Its effect on ln(N + 0.01) is below 10⁻¹⁰, and nothing tests `N == 0`. It is recorded here; nothing changes.
