### Scope

**In:**

   - `None` raises `ValueError` in every guarded function. A spec without a bound is refused, never silently unguarded (PRIME.4).
2. **The guard rule.** It accepts an orbit iff every coordinate of every state is finite and max_t |s_t[0]| ≤ B, with B closed. The guard reads the first coordinate (`observed_index`, which is 0 for all three systems), as WU-0007 Log l. 293 reads it: z exceeds B on the attractor for Lorenz-63 (47.45) and Chua (3.64).
   - A refusal raises a new `OrbitDivergence(RuntimeError)`. The message names the system, the orbit (`lyapunov`, `statistics`, `main` or `estimator`), the max |x| (or "non-finite"), B, and the first step that fails.
3. **Benettin.** `systems.lyapunov_guarded(spec, interval, n_steps, burn_in) -> (lambda_max, orbit_max_abs)`. It is the existing body plus a running max |x| and a first-failure index carried in the scan, over the final burn-in state and every averaging state (WU-0002.11 (5)).

   - `estimate_lyapunov` keeps its signature and delegates, returning `[0]`. This is the `estimate_counted` pattern of WU-0002.11a.

   - A legacy flat-float entry is recomputed once under the guard, and its IEEE bytes are asserted equal to the cached value. On a mismatch it raises (the regression check of DEC-0017 Q1). Otherwise the entry is rewritten in the new form.
   - An entry already in the new form is read without recomputation.
5. **The three `build_context` trajectories.** Each whole simulated array is guarded, burn-in included: statistics (`burn + 200 000`), main and estimator.
6. **New `ctx.meta` leaves**, which become `context` leaves through `experiments.context_record`: `lyapunov_orbit_max_abs` (float) and `trajectory_max_abs: {statistics, main, estimator}` (floats). That is 4 leaves per system.

### M1 Escape modes against the bounds: confirmed

**Chua.** For |x| > 1 the field is affine.
- The outer matrix has eigenvalues −6.073 and 0.3055 ± 4.525i.
- The x-weight of the unstable eigenvector is 0.355 of its norm. So |x| grows with the spiral amplitude at e^{0.3055 t}.
- Going from |x| = 3 to overflow takes at least ln(1e308/3)/0.3055 ≈ 2300 time units, which is about 30 000 study steps. **The bound is always crossed long before overflow.**

**Rössler.** Every divergence passes through |x| > 25 first.
- Escape needs z' = b + z(x − c) > 0 with x > c, and the large z then drives x' = −y − z far negative.
- On 6 segments bisected to 1e-15, the divergence boundary lies where the non-diverging side already exceeds |x| = 25 transiently, so the bound is conservative.

**Measured first failing steps** at the study Δ = 1/(30λ) (`esc_out.txt`, `bisect_out.txt`, `bisect_rossler_out.txt`, `pplus_out.txt`):

| System | Starts | Refused / diverged | First failing step: median / max | Worst case near the basin boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chua | 4000, uniform in \|x\|≤3, \|y\|≤1, \|z\|≤6 | 3015 / 3015; no divergence unrefused | 5 / 123 | 767 steps (59 time units), at 1e-16 from the boundary, 6 segments |
| Rössler | 4000, uniform in ±25 per coordinate | 1566 / 1496; no divergence unrefused | 1 / 105 | ≤ 396 steps from 1e-10 off the saddle-focus P⁺ = (5.69, −28.46, 28.46) (real eigenvalue 0.193); ≈ 450 extrapolated to float resolution |
| Lorenz-63 | — | no escape for the ODE (ball of radius 39.25) | — | — |

**Conclusion.** Every escape is refused within at most about 770 steps. That is less than the 3000-step burn-in of every `build_context` trajectory, so it is refused before any used state, and far less than the smoke estimator orbit (3000 + 25 001 + history). In the Benettin orbit (interval 0.1 or 0.8) the same times are at most 590 or 232 steps, less than the burn-in of 5000. Overflow never precedes the crossing.

**Side findings (no action needed):**
- **Rössler false refusals.** 70 of 4000 far Rössler starts exceed 25 transiently (up to 26.1) and then relax to the attractor. The refusal is conservative and unreachable from on-attractor starts.
- **Float-stationary equilibria.** Within about 1e-13 of an off-origin equilibrium the float RK4 map is stationary: the increment rounds away. 200 of 200 starts at 1e-14 from Rössler P⁺ stayed there for 3000 steps with |x| = 5.69, passing the guard. This mode is bounded and off the attractor, so a bound cannot see it. It has measure zero and cannot be reached from attractor states (see M5).

### M2 Placement of the Benettin guard: tighten (carry the guard in the burn-in loop as well)

**Mechanism of DEF-0015** (`m2_nan_jacobian.py`).
- JAX differentiates |x| at NaN as the x < 0 branch: d|x|/dx = −1.
- So once a Chua orbit overflows to NaN, `jacfwd(step_fn)` is the finite outer-region Jacobian. Benettin returns its unstable rate, 0.30550, which is the F1 value 0.3055.
- Hence λ alone can never reveal divergence.

**Recommendation.** Check every state k = 0 … burn_in + n_steps − 1, carried as (running max |x|, first-failure index) in the burn-in `fori_loop` and in the averaging scan.
- Use an explicit predicate ¬(all finite ∧ |x| ≤ B). Do not rely on NaN propagation in `max`. (It does propagate on CPU, `maximum(1, nan) = nan`, but the predicate is the specification.)
- The final scan carry (index burn_in + n_steps) does not enter λ and need not be checked.

**Evidence that λ is bitwise unchanged** (`m2_out.txt`, `m2_out_4threads.txt`, `m3_verify_out.txt`). The verbatim 529b90b loop was compared with variant A (carry in the scan only; the plan) and variant B (carry in the burn-in as well).
- **11 cases, all bitwise equal for A and B against the old loop:**
  - three systems × (n_steps, burn) ∈ {(10⁵, 5000), (1000, 50), (20 000, 1000)} at 1 thread;
  - the three production cases again at 4 threads;
  - the diverging Chua start of M3 (λ = 0.30547112037677077 in all three loops).
- The old loop reproduces the bits of `pilot_results_v1_local_baseline.json` on this host: 0.9054940534657817, 0.07193978510879935, 0.43113260161336364. V4 is therefore expected to pass.
- **Limit.** This is evidence on this CPU and XLA build, not a proof. XLA may change fusion or FMA contraction when outputs are added. The CPU evidence does not transfer to GPU, where contraction is on by default. G4 and V4 remain the gates.
- **Owner information.** A legacy `lyapunov.json` written by a GPU run and migrated on GPU has no evidence. The bitwise assertion would then fail loudly, not silently.
