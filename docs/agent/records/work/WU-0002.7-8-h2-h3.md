### Scope

**In.**
- **Shared helpers in `statistics.py`.** All are additive; `block_bootstrap`, `logrank_test` and `holm_adjust` keep their exact behavior.
  - `block_resample_indices(n_items, n_resamples, block, seed) -> Iterator[np.ndarray]`. It yields the same index arrays as `block_bootstrap`, from the same RNG calls in the same order.
  - `bootstrap_values(statistic, n_items, n_resamples, block, seed) -> tuple[float, np.ndarray]`. It returns the point estimate and the raw resample values.
  - `percentile_interval(values, level, unfavourable: Literal["low", "high"]) -> tuple[float, float, int]`. It returns the lower bound, the upper bound and the NaN count. NaN resamples are placed at the unfavourable tail, so a bound is infinite when the NaN fraction reaches α/2 (DEC-0008 A3).
  - `logrank_parts(...) -> tuple[float, float]`. It returns O − E and the variance, so a caller can see the zero-variance case that `logrank_test` maps to 1.0 (statistics.py:95-96). Counting that case stays with WU-0002.11.
- **Per-draw error correlation in `ensemble.py`.**
  - `draw_correlation(cp_err, plp_err) -> tuple[corr, cp_constant, plp_constant]` works on arrays of shape `(n, M, nH)` with a two-pass Pearson formula.
  - `cp_constant` is exact: `np.ptp(cp_err, axis=1) == 0`. Where CP's error is constant, `corr` is 0.0 (Methods v2 §M11 H2f).
  - `EnsembleMoments` gains `err_corr`, `cp_constant` and `plp_constant`, each `(n, nH)`. They are carried by `subset`, by `pipeline._concat_moments` and by `experiments._sweep_subset` (its column loop). No existing moment changes.
- **WU-0002.7 (`analysis.py`).**
  - `h2b_knob_response(noise_cells, library_cells, config)`:
    - (i) `noise` is unchanged (salt 3, bitwise).
    - (ii) `library_change` is the mean over states of w\*ᵢ(H_ref, 10⁴) − w\*ᵢ(H_ref, 10³) at ρ = 1%. It has an interval at `PRIMARY_LEVEL`, keys `mean_change`, `ci`, `level`, `n_states`, `pass = lo > 0` (strict, DEC-0008 A1) and `inconclusive_reason`.
    - `library_curves[ρ][n_lib]` gives the mean and median w\* with 95% intervals, for every ρ in `library_sweep_noises`. This is the M2 curve, reported only.
    - `library_change_reported["0.02"]` is the 10³ → 10⁴ change at ρ = 2% at 95%, reported only (§M12).
    - Removed: the per-ρ library Spearman and `median_at_largest_n_lib < 0.95` (D-05; M2 §M14).
    - `pass = (i) and (ii)`.
  - New `h2e_noise_matching(library_cells, config)`:
    - For each ρ in {0.01, 0.02}, the median over states of `err_corr`(H_ref, 10⁵) − `err_corr`(H_ref, 10³), with a 95% interval and `pass = lo > 0` (strict).
    - `corr_curve[ρ][n_lib]` gives the median correlation for every available n_lib.
    - `zero_variance_corr[ρ][n_lib]` is the count of CP-constant states at H_ref. It is returned in the result; `handling.*` belongs to WU-0002.11.
    - `pass` requires both ρ.
    - A missing ρ or n_lib (the smoke preset has neither 10⁵ nor ρ = 2%) gives a value of `None` and an `inconclusive_reason` naming what is missing. No NaN is fabricated.
- **WU-0002.8 (`analysis.py`).** `h3_regimes` keeps its signature and calls three new pure helpers.
  - `h3a_logrank(first_regime, durations, events, horizons_km, horizon_target, lt) -> dict`:
    - The first evaluation draw is used (DEC-0008 A4(f)). The pair order follows the KM horizon, as now.
    - Keys: `chosen_K`, `pairs`, `logrank_p_raw`, `logrank_p_holm`, `n_per_regime`, `iqr_lt` (per regime), `iqr_lt_mean` (the old `mean_iqr_lyapunov` value), `pass` and `inconclusive_reason`.
    - `pass` means every one of the K_real − 1 consecutive pairs was computed with variance > 0, and all Holm p < 0.01.
    - Otherwise the result is inconclusive with its reason. There is no `fail`, because no margin is set (**amended per DEC-0008 A3**).
    - No `regret_*` or `iqr_pass` key remains.
    - A missing or degenerate pair enters Holm with p = 1 and is reported as raw `None`.
  - `h3b_importances(con_profile, w_target, curvature, t_target, config) -> dict`:

    - `_variant_summary` is updated to the new signature.

### Applied-mathematician must confirm before execution

- **M5. H2e.**
  - Is two-pass Pearson over the M oracle draws right, with CP-constant defined as exact equality across draws? The pilot recheck's median of 6e-9 at 10³ suggests that moment subtraction leaves residue.
  - Is the extension right: PLP-constant (with CP varying) also set to 0 and counted separately?
  - Is E1's 1e-12 right?
- **M6. H3a Holm family.** Is the family all K_real − 1 consecutive pairs, with a missing or degenerate pair at p = 1 for the adjustment?
- **M7. The unfavourable-tail rule.** Is the exact rule "NaN → ±inf by pass side; a bound is infinite when either order statistic it interpolates is infinite", so that inf·0 is never NaN? For the band [0, 0.2), is the unfavourable side high?
- **M8. Tolerances.** C1's D ≥ 0.9 bound and exact zeros, and C2's bounds.
- **M9. H2c.** Is the choice by realized calibration MSE on the evaluation draws, with A0 on a tie, right?
