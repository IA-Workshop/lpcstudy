### Scope

**In.**
1. **Recording inside the same compiled loop** (`StateEstimator._build`, `refine`). Per estimate, return four extra outputs from the existing `jax.jit(jax.vmap(single))`:
   - `residual`: the final carried cost c_u, i.e. ‖X_t − G′(û)‖², sum of D = 7 squared terms in normalized observation units (σ_x = 1). It is already in the scan carry and discarded today.
   - `accepted`: the number of iterations k ≤ `refine_steps` with `c_cand < c_u` (the existing `accept`).
   - `nonfinite_cost` (S3): at least one of the 1 + `refine_steps` cost evaluations was non-finite. Read from `isinf(c)` on the mapped value: `cost` maps exactly the non-finite raw values to inf, and a sum of squares cannot be −inf.
   - `nonfinite_jacobian` (S4): at least one Jacobian entry was zeroed in some iteration.

   **How the arithmetic is kept unchanged.**
   - The carry gains integer fields only.
   - The float expressions for u, c_u, damping, `a_mat`, `normal`, `rhs`, `scale`, `delta`, `cand`, `c_cand` and `accept` keep their form and their order.
   - The `isfinite(a_mat)` mask is bound once and feeds both the existing `where` and the count.
   - `cost`, `generate`, `advance`, `nu_fn` and the jit/vmap boundary are untouched.
   - No separate pass. A second pass would leave the production function bitwise intact by construction, but it would re-run all 15 iterations, roughly doubling the LM cost, and it is the fallback only (see Risks).
   - Cost of the chosen design: four scalars per estimate, plus integer adds on masks the loop already computes. That is under 1% of the 15 `jacfwd` evaluations and 30 window integrations per estimate. The smoke wall time is recorded before and after, with no criterion.
   - **Trace variant for tests only:** the same body with scan `ys = (u_k, c_k, accept_k)`. It is never called by the pipeline.
2. **Return path.**
   - `estimate_counted` also returns an `LMDiagnostics` holding `residual (n,)` float64, `accepted (n,)` int, and `nonfinite_cost` and `nonfinite_jacobian (n,)` bool. `estimate` is unchanged.
   - `DRAW_COUNTERS` gains `lm_nonfinite_cost` and `lm_nonfinite_jacobian`. Their unit is one estimate and their denominator is `estimates`. They flow into `CELL_COUNTERS`, the totals and the `run.log` lines through the existing machinery.
3. **Per-estimate arrays.**
   - `DrawBatch`, `EnsembleMoments` and `EvaluationDraws` gain `lm_residual` and `lm_accepted`, shaped `(n, M)` or `(n, E)`. Both default to `None`.
   - `subset`, `_concat_moments`, `_concat_evals` and `_sweep_subset` slice them by rows. They are horizon-free.
   - The V3 and V4 check paths summarize their own batches and keep no arrays.
4. **Summary.** A pure `lm_summary(residual, accepted) -> dict` with exactly these keys:
   - `n_estimates`;
   - `median_residual` (`np.median`);
   - `p95_residual` (`np.quantile(·, 0.95)`, linear);
   - `median_iterations` (median of `accepted`);
   - `fraction_no_decrease` (mean of `accepted == 0`);
   - `nonfinite_residuals` (count of inf).

   A non-finite quantile is reported as `None`. With no estimates, `n_estimates` is 0 and the four values are `None`.
5. **Schema.**
   - `handling.cells[<id>]["lm"] = {**summary over all kinds, "by_kind": {"oracle": S, "eval": S, "check": S}}`, for every cell and check block (`V3-cp-horizon`, `V4-convergence`). Robustness cells get the same block through the same record.
   - It is not summed into `totals`, because `sum_totals` reads `counts` only.
   - `HANDLING_SCHEMA` becomes `lpcstudy.handling/2` (O1).
   - No hypothesis key changes, so `RESULT_COUNT_KEYS` is untouched. `tools/compare_pilot.py` already classifies `handling/**` as new.
6. **Report row.** RESULTS.md gets "### Levenberg–Marquardt diagnostics (verification, NVVUQ.10; not a hypothesis)" after the P2(c) subsection.
   - One row per system, cell and kind with n > 0. The columns are: cell id, kind, n, median residual, p95 residual, median accepted steps (of `estimator_refine_steps`), fraction with no decrease, and non-finite residuals.
   - One sentence states the stopping criterion (a fixed 15 iterations, with no tolerance stop) and the residual's definition and units.
   - A results file without the block gets one line, not an exception.
7. **Docstrings.** The `StateEstimator` Notes say that S3 and S4 are now counted.

### Applied-mathematician must confirm before execution
- **M1. Residual.** Is the final carried objective c (sum of squares, normalized units) the right reported quantity, rather than the RMS √(c/D) or c/ρ²?
  - Reading guide, not a criterion: with the exact model in the correct basin and converged, c ≈ ρ²χ²₄ to first order (D − m = 4), so the median ≈ 3.36ρ² and p95 ≈ 9.49ρ².
  - Should the report print c/ρ² beside it? No new results key unless confirmed.
- **M2. Non-increase.** The carried cost is monotone exactly, by construction: accept iff `c_cand < c_u` strictly, and the carry takes `c_cand` only then. So L1(a) tests the bookkeeping, not the numerics.
  - Confirm L2 as the numerical check, its relative 10⁻¹² and δ.
  - Note: the initial cost is computed outside the scan. A null step whose in-scan cost is an ulp lower than that initial value counts as accepted. Is that right ("accepted by the algorithm")?
- **M3. 10⁻¹²·D.** The expected value from the true state is about D·(κε‖x‖)², near 10⁻²⁸, so 7·10⁻¹² catches only gross errors. Tighten?
- **M4. `fraction_no_decrease`** (accepted = 0). With a fixed budget and damping ×4 on each rejection (10⁻³·4¹⁵ ≈ 10⁶), what does it mean? Does NVVUQ.10's "stopping criterion and achieved residual" need a convergence indicator for a fixed-budget stop (for example, accepted at iteration 15, or the relative decrease of the last accepted step)? The default is not to add one.
- **M5. Quantiles with inf.** Linear interpolation gives NaN for inf − inf. Confirm `None` plus the count, or choose an order-statistic method.
- **M6. Counter units and names.** Per-estimate booleans, or counts per evaluation or per entry? The inventory names `nonfinite_candidates` and `jacobian_nonfinite_entries` would misdescribe a per-estimate unit (DEC-0017 Q4 precedent); `lm_nonfinite_cost` and `lm_nonfinite_jacobian` are proposed.
- **M7. Reproducibility.**
  - Residuals and counts are leaves of the PLP chain. They are bitwise only on the same host, with the same `LPC_THREADS` (DEF-0023) and the same BLAS kernel (DEF-0024: W and U enter `generate` through `root`). Across hosts they need the PLP-chain tolerance.
  - `accepted` can flip at round-off ties, so `median_iterations` and `fraction_no_decrease` are not bitwise across hosts either.
  - Propose their cross-host tolerance for WU-0003.4. It is not fixed here.
