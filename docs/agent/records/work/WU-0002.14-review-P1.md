## Findings

**R1. Medium: route to the applied mathematician. Not a blocker for the reference.** The M5(d) margin claim for V6 does not survive the redraw. `systems/*/validation/integrator_max_dev_sigma`, P0 → P1:

| System | P0 | P1 | Change | Margin to the 1e-3 bar |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lorenz-63 | 3.93e-5 | **4.83e-4** | ×12.3 | 2.07× |
| Chua | 7.54e-5 | 2.92e-4 | ×3.9 | 3.4× |
| Rössler | 3.31e-5 | 4.25e-5 | | |

- M5(d) states that the V6 margin is "≥ 13×" and that P(fail) under a redraw is "≈ 0". It puts the whole 5 to 15% N4 risk on V4.
- One redraw already brings Lorenz-63 to 2.07×. V6 is the maximum over only 20 test states over 1 LT (`validation.check_integrator`), so its law across seeds is heavy-tailed, and the confirmatory seed draws new test states.
- Similarly, the Lorenz V2 per-state minimum is 0.838 against the M5(d) note "per-state min 0.91". The gate reads the median (1.011), so this is information only.
- Chua's V4 margin is now ×1.19 (0.0420), inside M5(d)'s stated V4 risk.
- **Why it matters.** The predicted chance of a gate failure on a fresh seed is understated for V6. A V6 failure on the confirmatory seed cannot be retuned (DEC-0027(3), PRIME.2).
- **Fix.** The applied mathematician re-estimates P(V6 fail) and P(V4 fail) per seed, for example over synthetic DEV seeds with the guarded production context and no study seed. The M5(d) table and the unit's risk R1 are amended. The V6 bar, the state count and the horizon do not change.
