  2. A unit test with an injected non-finite resample (monkeypatch the statistic or the resample values) showing that the failed resample goes to the A3 tail and `n_failed_resamples` equals the injected count, and that the verdict becomes `inconclusive` when the count reaches the tail threshold `floor((B - 1) a) + 1` with `a = (1 - level)/2` (DEC-0012).
