**2026-10-11, applied-mathematician (claude-opus-5-5): mathematics review of the plan (before code).** Scratch CPU scripts only (2 threads; `scratchpad/v7math/hand.py`, `mc.py`, 400 replications each, rng 12345); no tests and no presets run. Model of the simulation: whitened 3-d error e = ρ·η_i·(1+κ_iρ)·L_i z (η_i lognormal sd 0.02; κ ~ |N(0.2, 0.1)|; 2% per-level jitter); z shared across levels (common random numbers, as `Study.unit_noise`); L_i rank-1 (worst case) or eigenvalue fractions (0.8, 0.15, 0.05); variants: no CRN, a floor δ² += (0.3·ρ_min·ν)², wrong-basin (WB) states nested in ρ.

1. **Estimator: per-state OLS slope, then the median over states. Correct, and it is the documented one** (M2 §M10: "median per-state slope"; v3's "median slope" reads the same way). A pooled OLS, with or without state intercepts, equals the *mean* of the per-state slopes on this balanced design, so a few WB states move it. The slope of the medians tests only population scaling, and the median state can change between levels. Levels: 2 at smoke (0.01, 0.02; Σd² = 0.2402), 4 at pilot and full (Σd² = 2.9036). Monte Carlo sd of the median slope with CRN: 0.0010-0.0012 (smoke: n = 60, M = 30), 0.0002 (pilot: n = 300, M = 200), 0.0001 (full: n = 1000, M = 200). Without CRN: 0.034-0.041, 0.0017-0.0020, 0.0010-0.0012. The pilot bootstrap half-widths (0.0002-0.001) agree. The sd of the per-ρ median efficiency: 0.016-0.021 (smoke), 0.0033-0.0042 (pilot), 0.0018-0.0022 (full); 0.007-0.008 at n = 60 with M = 200. The median bias of sqrt(mean χ²/M) is -0.011 at M = 30 (rank-1) and -0.0037 for an isotropic Σ; it is -0.0017 at M = 200. **The planner's 0.02 and -0.01 are confirmed as the smoke worst case.** Against the 0.1 margin this is at least 4.5σ at smoke and at least 20σ at pilot and full, so the band is meaningful. Bias sources: a floor shows up first in the efficiency at the smallest ρ (φ = 0.46 gives efficiency 1.10 at 0.5%, while the slope is still 0.96). WB states inflate the efficiency at large ρ (10% of states at 1%, scaling ∝ ρ, gives 1.13-1.16 at 5%) and barely move the median slope. Under CRN the slope is nearly tautological (M13.6). **The per-ρ efficiency is the binding part of V7, and "at every ρ" is essential.**

So V7, and therefore `gate_pass`, will very likely FAIL at smoke, and (xi) "gate_pass is true" will not hold. The cause is estimator quality, not sampling error (sd 0.02). The plan's own branch applies: record, DEF, `/refer`, no tuning.
2. **Efficiency = median over states of δ_i/(ρν_i), with each state's own oracle ν_i: sound.** The Cramér-Rao bound is per state (ρ²ν_i², with ν_i² = tr(JᵀJ)⁻¹ in whitened coordinates). The pilot medians of 1.000-1.014 and V2 confirm that δ and ν use the same coordinates. **Test-design defects, tightened:**
   - (ii) ν_i = 0.5, 1, …, 5.5 is arithmetic, so median(ν)/mean(ν) = 1 exactly, and the mean-ν variant also gives 1: the check is vacuous. Use ν_i = 2^((i−5)/2), i = 0…10, where median/mean = 0.5824 fails the band.
   - (v) Pair the efficiencies (0.80, 0.95, 1.0, 1.05, 2.0) with ν = (2, 4, 0.5, 1, 8), using exactly 5 states (odd n). The ratio of medians is then 0.80 (fail), against a median of ratios of 1.0 (pass).
   - **(vii) Tighten:** `n_nonfinite` also counts states with a non-finite or non-positive ν_i. Note that `ParametricEstimator.estimate` already replaces non-finite estimates with `state_mean` (estimation.py, the last lines of `estimate`), uncounted. So δ is finite even after a divergence, and (vii) cannot see it. This is PRIME.4, out of scope: refer it.
   - `block_bootstrap` uses `nanquantile`, so NaN resamples are dropped silently from the reported interval.
3. **Decide on point medians; report the intervals.** E0 is a verification gate (V2 uses a point median, V4 a point half-width). DEC-0008 A1 governs the §2 hypotheses. At pilot and full sizes the sampling sd is ≤ 0.004, so a point rule and an interval rule give the same verdict unless the median lies within about 0.01 of an edge. Consequence of an interval rule: a third, inconclusive state that the boolean gate cannot carry, and smoke values near an edge (1.096 ± 0.04) would fail.
