### Acceptance criteria (fixed now, VV.3; the mathematician may tighten, never loosen)

**Moment generator (realizable by construction).** Draw (c, u, V_u) and set:
- E_C = c(1 − u)² + V_u;
- E_P = c·u² + V_u;
- C = (E_C + E_P − c)/2.

Then E_C·E_P − C² = c·V_u ≥ 0. The reference sees only (E_C, E_P, C).

Ranges:
- c is log-uniform on [10⁻³, 10];
- V_u/c is uniform on [0, 10];
- u is stratified: 70 draws in (−5, 0), 60 in [0, 1], 70 in (1, 6).

The seed is `np.random.default_rng(<int>)`, fixed in the test.

### APPLIED-MATHEMATICIAN must confirm before execution

- **Q1:** which excess form to use. `c[(w−u)² − (w\*−u)²]` is ≥ 0 exactly in IEEE arithmetic because rounding is monotone, but it cancels for large \|u\|. The factored form does not cancel, but its sign is not guaranteed near w\*. Is (a′) "≥ 0" the right requirement?
- **Q2:** the grid argument of (a): h = 1/9999, the bound c(h/2)², and "excess ≥ c(w − w\*)²" in clipped cases.
- **Q3:** the two-stage grid reference and the flatness bound in (b). Is this a valid reading of "on the same grid"?
- **Q4:** the (c, u, V_u) generator and its ranges, as the conditioning limits for (a) and (b).
- **Q5:** P3(a) over (state, draw) pairs, since regime assignment is per draw. Is Σ(E_P − C)/Σc the right numerical form? What about the `1e-300` floor?
