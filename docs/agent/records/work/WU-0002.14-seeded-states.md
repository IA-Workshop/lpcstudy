### Scope

**In (a): start states (DEF-0017; Methods v2 change-list item 8; v3 "Same attractors, fresh seeds").** The changes are in `data.build_context` and a new pure function `data.draw_starts`.
- One generator `make_rng(seed, "start", system)` is consumed in a fixed order: k_main, u_main, k_est, u_est.
  - k is uniform on {0, …, 199 999}, an index into the post-burn-in statistics orbit.

  - `start_index: {main, estimator}` (int);
  - `start_state: {main: [3], estimator: [3]}` (float).

### M1 Start distribution

**(a) Closeness to the invariant measure.** Let μ_N be the empirical measure of the post-burn statistics orbit. The start law is ν = μ_N ∗ N(0, η²C), and after b = 3000 steps the law is Φ^b_*ν.

Hypotheses:
- **(H1)** μ_N ≈ μ, the physical measure of the RK4 map. The sampling error is measured in M6: SD of the reference ECDF at the median is 0.006 for Lorenz and Chua and 0.0003 for Rössler.
- **(H2)** The η-neighbourhood of the sampled orbit lies in the basin (b) below.
- **(H3)** μ is Φ-invariant.

Argument: by (H3), if η = 0 then Φ^b_*μ_N is μ_N shifted by b, which is within b/N = 0.015 of μ_N in KS. With η > 0 the perturbation saturates within about 14 LT (table (c)), and the post-burn state is a fresh point. Its law is still the mixture over k of states pushed forward from μ_N.
- **No mixing within the burn-in is needed.** The uniform index carries μ_N's phase and lobe distribution. That is what makes it work for the phase-coherent Rössler.
- **Measured.** These are iid two-sample KS tests at n = 4000 post-burn x, which avoid the reference's autocorrelation:

| System | on-orbit vs on-orbit | on-orbit vs independent long orbit | **registry + η vs on-orbit** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lorenz-63 | D 0.014, p 0.84 | D 0.016, p 0.26 | D 0.021, p 0.33 |
| Rössler | D 0.019, p 0.50 | D 0.012, p 0.60 | **D 0.396, p ≈ 1e-280** |
| Chua | D 0.015, p 0.76 | D 0.015, p 0.34 | D 0.011, p 0.96 |

**The registry-plus-η alternative fails for Rössler.** After 100 LT the phase is still concentrated: the mean resultant length of atan2(y, x) is R = 0.85, against 0.07 under the on-orbit scheme. The whitened spread is about 0.5. Seeds' main trajectories would be phase-locked with each other for hundreds of LT, which defeats "different seeds, different trajectories". Lorenz and Chua mix fast enough for either scheme.

**(b) Refusal probability.** The escape fraction p(ε) after the burn-in, as a function of the whitened perturbation size ε, with starts on the orbit:

| ε | 1e-6 | 1e-4 | 3e-3 | 1e-2 | 1.5e-2 | 2e-2 | 3e-2 | 0.1 | 0.15 | 0.2 | 0.3 | 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lorenz-63 (n 2·10⁴) | 0 | 0 | | 0 | | | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 1e-4 (starts above B = 40, not escapes) |
| Rössler (n 2·10⁵ at ≤ 0.03) | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | **1.5e-5** | 5.0e-4 | 1.6e-2 (2·10⁴) | 2.7e-2 | 3.4e-2 | 4.7e-2 | 0.10 |
| Chua (n 2·10⁵ at 0.1 to 0.2) | 0 | 0 | | 0 | | | 0 | 0 | 0 | **1.2e-4** | 2.8e-3 (2·10⁴) | 0.18 |

- The onset is very steep: Rössler's local exponent is about 9 between 0.02 and 0.03. This is the signature of a positive gap between the attractor and the escape set (Rössler about 0.015 to 0.02 whitened, Chua about 0.15 to 0.2), not a power law reaching down to 0.
- **The binding system is Rössler** (escape to infinity through x > c), not Chua.
- This agrees with the independent .11c check, M5(c) of WU-0002.11c-orbit-guard.md, commit d3acd5b, which used different scripts and seeds: 0/10⁴ at ε ≤ 0.02 and the first escape at 0.03 for Rössler. - **Bound at η = 1e-6.** Assume **(H-mono)**: the escape fraction does not increase as ε decreases, which holds whenever escape requires crossing the gap. Then P(start escapes) ≤ p(0.015) < 1.5e-5 (95%, 0/2·10⁵), with a margin of 1.5·10⁴ in ε. Under the gap hypothesis it is 0: the offset is η‖u‖, and ‖u‖ > 10⁴ is impossible in practice.
- **Escape along the guarded orbit** (chaotic-saddle check, no hypothesis): 20 000 on-orbit starts per system were run for 10⁵ steps and then for 10⁶ steps (result below). Max|x| matches the statistics orbit to 2·10⁻⁵ (Rössler 11.4332, Chua 2.26309).

  - Result: **0 refusals in 2.2·10¹⁰ steps per system**, so the 95% bound on the escape rate is 1.4e-10 per step.

  - Lorenz-63 is rigorous: the trapping ball of radius 39.25 lies inside B = 40, and the observed max|x| is 19.7 over 1.4·10⁸ steps.

### M5 Regression (D3, SE arm, R-f, gate margins)

**(a) Is D3 valid for a redrawn sample?**
- D3 needs: Δ = θ̂_new − θ̂_old with Var Δ = SE_old² + SE_new², the SEs correct, and the runs independent.
- Independence holds approximately. The states, the estimator training trajectory and the networks differ. The DEV-0 noise arrays are identical but applied to different states, which gives at most a weak positive correlation and so a conservative error.
- Two effects inflate the true SD over the bootstrap SE by a factor κ:
  - DEC-0018 undercoverage: 95% coverage of 0.92 to 0.947 gives κ = 1.01 to 1.12.
  - **Variance the state bootstrap does not capture**, from refitted networks, rules (A2 to A5), the library and K. The bootstrap conditions on these (DEC-0024: "conditional on one trajectory, library and network realization"). A redraw resamples them. Equal components would give κ ≈ 1.41. For Q-N ΔR² this component may dominate (DEC-0018 O5).

**(b) SE arm (DEC-0020, open).**
- By the bootstrap principle, L(θ̂* − θ̂) ≈ L(θ̂ − θ). Take Δ > 0:
  - the right tail of θ̂_new − θ is estimated by new's **upper** arm, which faces away from old;
  - the left tail of θ̂_old − θ is estimated by old's **lower** arm, which also faces away from new.
- **Recommendation: both arms facing away from the other estimate.** The redraw makes the two runs exchangeable, and this rule is symmetric under swapping them.
- The implemented rule uses old's arm on the side of Δ, which faces toward new: the CI logic of N3, where the pilot is the reference. It is not symmetric under the swap.
- An acceptable alternative is the half-width (hi − lo)/(2z) on both sides: symmetric, with lower Monte Carlo noise, and indifferent to percentile against basic.
- Upper/lower arm ratios in the current pilot range from 0.78 to 1.77 (median 0.99), so the choice affects only a few statistics. **Owner decision (O4), before P1.**

### M7 JAX keys and draw order

- **32-bit keys.** jax 0.11.2 (threefry2x32, `jax_threefry_partitionable=True`) accepts `PRNGKey(int)` for every value in [0, 2³²) with x64 on and off; both settings were tested. The key is [0, v]. Use `int(rng.integers(0, 2**32))`.
  - About 130 keys per full run collide with probability about 2e-6, and a collision would only share an init or shuffle stream between networks trained on different data. **Acceptable.**

- **Draw order.** Any fixed order is deterministic, and P7 holds. Recommended: **draw the two JAX keys first**, then the permutation, then the validation noise.
